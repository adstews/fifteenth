Equity Planning

Your compensation is complex. Your tax strategy should be too.

Most CPAs treat equity like any other income. We build personalized strategies around every vesting schedule, exercise window, and liquidity event.

⚖

RSUs & Restricted Stock

RSU vesting triggers taxable income at market value. We model the optimal timing for selling vested shares, coordinate with your overall income to minimize your effective tax rate, and plan around upcoming vesting cliffs.

⚙

Stock Options (ISOs & NSOs)

The difference between ISOs and NSOs can mean tens of thousands in tax liability. We calculate AMT exposure, model exercise timing scenarios, and build multi-year strategies so you exercise at the right time for the right cost.

↹

Vesting Cliffs & Schedules

Whether you hit a 6-month, 1-year, or 2-year cliff, the moment equity starts flowing changes everything. We plan ahead so you're ready before your first shares vest — not scrambling after.

★

409A Valuations & FMV

Pre-IPO equity is valued at the company's 409A fair market value — which can change dramatically between rounds. We track these shifts and time your decisions around them.

❖

AMT Planning

ISO exercises at pre-IPO companies can trigger Alternative Minimum Tax. We run AMT projections before you exercise so you know the exact cost — and whether it makes sense this year or next.

⇄

Liquidity Event Readiness

When your company goes public or gets acquired, the clock starts ticking. We prepare your tax strategy months in advance so you can act decisively when the window opens.

Tender Offers

A tender offer opens. You have 30 days. Are you ready?

Tender offers give pre-IPO employees a rare window to sell shares before a public listing. The window is short — typically 30 days — and the tax implications are significant. We make sure you're prepared before it opens.

01

Pre-tender modeling

We run scenarios before the offer is announced. How much should you sell? What's the tax hit? How does it interact with your other income this year? You'll have answers before the clock starts.

02

Tax impact analysis

Tender offer proceeds are taxed differently depending on your grant type, holding period, and cost basis. We calculate the exact federal and state tax liability for every scenario so there are no surprises.

03

Decision framework

Sell everything? Sell some? Hold for a potential IPO? We help you weigh liquidity needs against tax efficiency and future upside — then give you a clear recommendation you can act on immediately.

Frequently asked questions

What types of equity compensation do you specialize in? +

We specialize in all forms of equity compensation including Restricted Stock Units (RSUs), Incentive Stock Options (ISOs), Non-Qualified Stock Options (NSOs), Performance Stock Units (PSUs), and Employee Stock Purchase Plans (ESPPs). Our team has deep experience with pre-IPO equity packages and the unique tax challenges they present.

How do you help with tender offers? +

When a tender offer is announced, the window is typically only 30 days. We prepare in advance by modeling different sell scenarios, calculating the tax impact of each option, and helping you decide how much to sell based on your financial goals, tax situation, and views on the company's future. Our clients go into tender offers with a clear plan, not last-minute guesswork.

I just hit my equity cliff. What should I do? +

Hitting your cliff means shares are starting to vest, which triggers tax events. The right strategy depends on your grant type, income level, state of residence, and financial goals. Book a consultation and we'll review your specific situation and build a plan tailored to your equity package and timeline.

What if my company hasn't announced a tender offer yet? +

That's the best time to plan. We work with you proactively — modeling potential scenarios, optimizing your exercise strategy, and positioning you to act quickly when a liquidity event does happen. Pre-planning is the key to maximizing your after-tax outcome.

How is pricing structured? +

We charge a flat annual fee based on the complexity of your tax situation. Equity planning, tax filing, year-round advisory, and unlimited messaging with your CPA are all included. No hourly billing. No surprise charges. You'll see your personalized price before you commit.

Do I need to switch my entire tax filing to Fifteenth? +

Yes — our equity planning is integrated with your full tax picture. To give you the most accurate advice on equity decisions, we need to see your complete income, deductions, and tax situation. This is how we find savings that other advisors miss.
